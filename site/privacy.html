Privacy Policy

health-data-connect

What we access

With your consent, health-data-connect requests read-only access to your Google Health data (such as activity and fitness, sleep, and heart-rate metrics) via Google's Health API. It never requests write access and never accesses your location.

Where your data goes

All access happens locally. Your OAuth tokens and any health data fetched are stored and processed on your device only. This application runs no server: we do not operate any backend, and your health data and tokens are never transmitted to us or to any third party. We never see, collect, store, or share your data.

How your data is used

Data is read solely to answer questions you ask in your own local AI assistant. It is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any other purpose, and it is not sold.

Revoking access

Run uvx health-data-connect disconnect to delete the local tokens, and revoke the grant entirely at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
