health-data-connect

Connect your Fitbit / Google Health data to a local AI assistant — in one command.

health-data-connect lets you talk to your own wearable health data (sleep, activity, heart rate, and more) inside a local MCP client such as Claude Code or Claude Desktop. You connect your Google account once; nothing is set up on your side beyond a single command.

How it works

uvx health-data-connect auth

This opens Google's consent screen, where you grant read-only access to your own health data. Your access tokens and data stay on your device — this project operates no server and never receives your health data.

Your privacy

We access only read-only health scopes, only to answer your own questions locally. See the full privacy policy. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
